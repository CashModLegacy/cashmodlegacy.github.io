# 😺 CashMod

Welcome to **CashMod**, the ultimate suite of tools — what we like to call **CashServices** — designed for creators, developers, and enthusiasts alike.  

CashMod is more than a single tool; it's a full collection of integrated utilities to enhance productivity, creativity, and security across your projects. Whether you're building software, managing Linux environments, or improving your Scratch workflow, CashMod has something for you.

Here’s a quick overview of the tools included in CashMod (All of them aren't complete yet, and are subject to change):

Each service is designed to work seamlessly together, providing a cohesive suite experience for all types of users.
